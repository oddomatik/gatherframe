# Your first delivery

Create a project in **Projects**. A new project is a private draft, defaults to a
simple gallery, and leaves print sales off. Choose story/sections or a collection
directory when it suits the shoot; existing projects keep their defaults.

The **First delivery guide** on the Photos tab reads saved project state. It does
not change settings, publish a gallery or send messages. Its four setup checks are:

1. **Upload:** at least one photo was imported. Start with a small export batch.
2. **Prepare:** at least one photo has a ready preview and belongs to an active,
   non-intake collection. Photos only in **To sort** remain private. A subset may
   be delivered while other photos are still being prepared.
3. **Open:** the project is published and its closing time has not passed.
4. **Access:** broad access is enabled, or an active scoped invitation includes
   at least one ready photo in an active collection. Revoked, expired and empty
   scopes do not satisfy the scoped-only check.

Use **Presentation** to choose layout, public titles and ordering. Review download
choices in **Settings** and private-client access in **Sharing**. A collection or
tag filter is not a permission boundary: use scoped invitations and explicitly
disable broad project links when clients must see only their own collections.

Before sending anything, open the intended guest link in a private browser window
and check its password gate, visible photos and any enabled download. An owner
preview is not proof of guest access. The guide's setup count is not recipient
verification, complete photo processing, delivery success or backup certification.

For proof selections, create a round for the invitation and explain the requested
selection to your client. Favorites are separate from submitted proof selections.
Print orders, payment settings and notifications are optional follow-on setup.

Self-hosting operators should also follow [setup](self-hosting.md) and the
[recovery and upgrade procedure](operations.md); protect both media and database.
