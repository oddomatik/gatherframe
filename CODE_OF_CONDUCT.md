# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and publishing another person’s
private photographs or information are not acceptable.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-managed spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, warn contributors or restrict participation.
They should explain decisions privately where appropriate and avoid conflicts of interest.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer using the contact listed on their GitHub profile.
If no private route is available, request one without naming affected people or publishing sensitive details.
The publishing maintainer must identify a private contact before opening community discussions.
